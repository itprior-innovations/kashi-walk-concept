What is verified?

Only that these records were returned by a small OpenStreetMap area extract on 23 November 2026. There are 19 named and 4 unnamed records. Some generic names may describe different small shrines; identities still need local review.

Distances follow an experimental local shortest-path calculation on OSM walkable ways. They include straight connector estimates to map points, not verified entrances. Time assumes 60 metres/minute, with no queue, worship, traffic or crowd allowance.

Road tagging does not prove public pedestrian access. Stairs, gates, closures and flood conditions can make a path unsuitable.

Scripture comes after verification

No Kashi Khanda verses or temple-text matches have been added yet. Each needs a chosen edition, an exact chapter/verse reference and rights review. We have not read the complete Kashi Khanda for this concept.
