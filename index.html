A map-first Kashi concept

Not field-verified. Not turn-by-turn navigation. 23 OSM temple records, including 19 named and 4 unnamed points. Gates, access, steps, hours and crowds are unverified. Generic records may need identity checks.

Walking estimates follow a local OSM path graph, with straight connectors to map points. Assumption: 60 metres/minute, excluding queues, worship and crowd delays. Road tags do not prove safe or public access.

No Kashi Khanda verse-to-temple matches are verified here. No sacred circuit is claimed. Scripture links require an edition and rights check.
